Profile

On breaking things to understand them

I study how systems fail, and how to make them fail more gracefully. My focus sits across penetration testing, red teaming, OSINT, and threat detection the practical edges of cybersecurity, where curiosity meets discipline.

The goal isn't just to break things. It's to understand them well enough to explain the failure clearly, and help build something sturdier in its place. This site is a short, deliberately quiet record of that work details are shared on my own terms, not left lying around for anyone to scrape.
