Privacy Policy

Nothing leaves
your phone.

Kite does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. This page explains exactly what that means, in plain language.

What we collect

We do not collect anything. Kite has no servers, so there is nothing for us to collect and nowhere for it to go. There is no sign up, no account, and no login.

What stays on your device

To work as your home screen, Kite needs to know which apps are installed on your phone, and it remembers small things like your search text and how many times you have opened an app.

All of this stays only on your phone, inside the app's own storage. None of it is sent anywhere, not even to us.

Permissions we ask for

Kite asks for a couple of permissions so it can work properly as your launcher.

  • See your installed apps, so it can show them in your app list.
    • Permission is automatically granted.
  • Delete apps, so the uninstall option inside Kite works.
    • Permission is automatically granted.
  • Default Home, so Kite is your default home launcher.

These permissions are only ever used for what they say above. Kite never reads your files, messages, contacts, photos, or anything else on your phone.

No ads, no trackers

Kite does not show ads. It does not use analytics tools, advertising kits, or any third party service that tracks what you do. Nothing about how you use the app is measured or sent out.

Links you choose to open

The menu inside Kite has links to this website and to our email address. If you tap one, your browser or mail app opens as usual. What happens after that follows the privacy policy of your browser or mail app, not this one.

Children's privacy

Kite does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Since the app does not collect data from anyone at all, that is true for users of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted right here on this page. Since Kite does not collect any data today, a future change would only make this promise stronger, never weaker.

Found a bug?

If Kite crashes, misbehaves, or you run into any problem, write to us. We read every email and aim to fix real issues within 2 business days. No data needed, just tell us what happened.
